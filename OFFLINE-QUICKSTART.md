# Start the Arduino Guide

This copy is ready to use. You do not need to install anything or open a terminal.

1. Extract `25-week-arduino-guide-offline.zip` to a folder you can find again.
2. Open that folder.
3. Double-click **START-HERE.html**.
4. If your computer asks, choose a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

The guide opens in your browser. Your parts checks and lesson progress are saved in that browser on this computer (using its localStorage), so they will still be there when you come back later.

If you switch browsers, use private/incognito mode, clear browser data, or move to another computer, your saved checkmarks may not come with you. To begin a lesson again, use that lesson's **Reset progress** button.

If double-clicking does not open the guide, open your browser first, choose **Open File**, and select **START-HERE.html** from the extracted folder.
